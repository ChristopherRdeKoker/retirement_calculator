# Retirement calculator

A small retirement calculator for South Africans. Enter your age, savings, contributions and any lump-sum deposits, and it shows how long your savings last, what your pot is worth at retirement and whether your desired income is sustainable. Up to three scenarios can be saved and compared side by side. Nothing leaves the browser.

Built with Rust compiled to WebAssembly, Leptos 0.8 and Trunk, with a hand-built SVG chart and plain CSS. Run it with `trunk serve` and test it with `cargo test`. It builds to static files under `/apps/retirement/`.

First version, built over a few days in November 2026.
